# Sales playbook — DT Restora

## Lead stages
| Stage | Meaning | Moves to next stage when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| new | first message | they ask about price, features or a demo |
| interested | asked price/features | they agree to a demo |
| demo | demo requested or booked | demo done and they ask for a proposal |
| proposal | price/proposal sent by Sakir | they pay or say no |
| won / lost | closed | — |

## What the assistant may do
- Answer product questions from the knowledge files.
- Share package prices exactly as written in `pricing.md`.
- Offer a free demo and collect name, restaurant name and phone number.
- Send one follow-up inside Meta's 24-hour window if a warm lead goes quiet.

## What always goes to Sakir
- Discounts, special prices, free months, refunds, contracts.
- Custom features or integrations.
- Complaints and existing-customer problems.
- Anything the knowledge files do not answer.

## Qualifying questions (ask one at a time)
1. How many branches/tables does your restaurant have?
2. Do you take online or delivery orders?
3. What do you use now (paper, another software)?
